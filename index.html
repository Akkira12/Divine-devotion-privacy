Privacy Policy

Effective September 14, 2026

Divine Devotion is a personal devotional and journaling app. This policy explains what information is stored on your device and what information may be collected by our advertising provider.

Information stored on your device

Journal entries and favorites you create in Divine Devotion stay on your device. They are not sent to or stored on our servers. Removing the app or clearing its app data may permanently delete this information.

Advertising and analytics

Divine Devotion uses Google AdMob to display advertising. Google AdMob may collect coarse location, device ID, product interaction, advertising, crash, and performance data. This information may be used for advertising and analytics, subject to Google’s policies and your device’s privacy settings.

For more information, please review Google’s Privacy Policy and the privacy controls available on your device.

Children’s privacy

Divine Devotion is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a service connected to the app, please contact us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
